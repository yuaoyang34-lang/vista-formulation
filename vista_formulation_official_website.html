Streamlined Production Roadmap

From Concept to Commercial Distribution

Our 5-stage manufacturing framework ensures complete traceability, batch consistency, and fast time-to-market.

Phase 01

Custom Formulation & R&D Consultation

Our chemical engineers and formulation scientists collaborate with your team to define active ingredient dosages, delivery mechanisms (capsules, gummies, powders), and flavor matrices. We audit your formula for stability, bioavailability, and regulatory compliance.

  • Flavor sample bench testing & refinement
  • Supplement Facts label verification
Pilot Sample Sign-off Receive benchtop samples for tasting & approval
Phase 02

Raw Material Sourcing & Analytical Testing

We procure pristine raw ingredients from vetted global suppliers. Every incoming lot is quarantined and subjected to FTIR identity testing, heavy metals screening (ICP-MS), and microbiological verification prior to release into manufacturing.

  • Full Certificate of Analysis (CoA) documentation
  • Non-GMO, Organic & Kosher certified ingredient chains
Raw Material Clearance 100% component verification before blending
Phase 03

Precision Blending & High-Speed Production

Utilizing climate-controlled cleanrooms, raw materials undergo sifting, precision weigh-ups, and high-shear V-blender mixing. Powder mixtures move to high-speed encapsulation, tableting presses, or gummy cookers.

  • Humidity & temperature controlled cleanroom suites
  • In-line weight, disintegration & hardness checks
Automated High-Speed Production Continuous monitoring & weight checks
Phase 04

Quality Control Inspection & Packaging

Finished dosage units are directed to automated packaging lines for HDPE bottling, blister packing, or pouch filling. Features induction tamper-evident seals, lot coding, and shrink sleeve application.

  • Laser vision inspection for label placement
  • Final finished product Certificate of Analysis (CoA)
Automated Bottling & Sealing Tamper-evident shrink bands & expiration coding
Phase 05

Final Release & Logistics Logistics

Palletized goods are safely stored in our climate-controlled warehouse until final QA release. We handle direct-to-fulfillment freight dispatch, Amazon FBA prep compliance, or international export documentation.

  • Pallet wrapping & heat-mapped logistics
  • Seamless dropship to master fulfillment hubs
Ready for Market Dispatch Full shipping manifest & tracking support
