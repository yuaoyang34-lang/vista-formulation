The 4-Step Turnkey Manufacturing Process

From initial benchtop formulation to finished retail-ready goods delivered with rigorous documentation.

01

R&D & Formulation

Consultation with expert food scientists and formulators to optimize ingredient synergy, bioavailability, flavor profiles, and FDA-compliant Supplement Facts label claims.

02

Sourcing & Testing

Procurement of high-purity raw ingredients and packaging components from vetted global suppliers, accompanied by thorough identity, potency, and purity lab assays.

03

Production & Packaging

High-efficiency batch blending, encapsulation or tableting, automated bottle/pouch filling, induction sealing, and custom labeling under strict cGMP protocols.

04

Logistics & Delivery

Finished product third-party lab clearance, Certificate of Analysis (COA) issuance, master case palletization, and secure freight delivery to your warehouse or 3PL.
